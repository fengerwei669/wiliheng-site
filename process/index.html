What the records are worth to a buyer

Steel heat number on every batch

We buy strip with a mill test report (MTR) and file the heat number against the batch. Your inspection documents can carry it.

Hardness measured, not assumed

Tempering is the step where a 500-piece order is won or lost. The measured HRC is written down per furnace load, and a batch outside the range does not move on.

A real third-party inspection

You are welcome to send SGS, BV or your own QC to the plant, or to audit the process route above point by point before you place the order.
