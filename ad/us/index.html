Common questions

What is the minimum order?

500 pcs per model for stock lines. For mixed assortments we look at the total quantity.

Can I get samples first?

Yes. Samples are charged, and the cost is normally credited against the production order.

How long is production?

Stock models 20–30 days; OEM/private label with your logo and packaging 30–45 days after approval, depending on quantity.

What payment terms do you accept?

Typically 30% deposit and 70% before shipment. We confirm terms with the order.

Do you ship with import documents?

Yes — commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and other papers as agreed.
