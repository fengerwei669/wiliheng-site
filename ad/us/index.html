Common questions

What is the minimum order?

500 pcs per model. For mixed assortments we look at the total quantity.

Can I get models and photos?

Yes — in the chat. We do not send a photo dump; we shortlist the models that fit your line with real photos and pricing.

Can I see the factory without flying to China?

Yes. We run a live video walkthrough (WhatsApp or Zoom, 15–20 minutes) through production — usually within 1–2 working days of your enquiry.

Can I get samples first?

Yes. Samples are charged and normally credited against the production order.

How long is production?

Stock models 20–30 days; OEM/private label with your logo and packaging 30–45 days after approval.

What payment terms do you accept?

Typically 30% deposit, 70% before shipment; we confirm terms with the order.

How big is the factory and what do you make in house?

Our factory in Yangjiang runs 43 production steps in house with 40 machines, including 20 CNC machining centres, 10 water grinding machines, 5 vertical grinders, 2 annealing and 2 tempering furnaces, plus Rockwell hardness testing. We manufacture for importers, distributors and brand owners.

Which materials are possible?

Stainless steel 5Cr15MoV and 440C, with other grades on request; handles in G10, wood, plastic or to your drawing. Packaging as colour box, blister or protective sleeve.

Which countries do you supply?

We produce for customers in the United States, Germany and across the EU — with export documents and retail-ready packaging. Support in English or German.

How fast do I get an answer?

A quote usually within 24 hours on working days, with a live video walkthrough of production on request. Samples for OEM/private label take about a week.
