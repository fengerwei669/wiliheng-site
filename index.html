What we do in-house

Everything that decides how a knife feels and lasts — we do it ourselves. No subcontracted workshops, no third party between you and the line.

Blades & heat treatment

Blanking, annealing and tempering in our own furnaces, followed by hardness verification on our Rockwell tester. Every batch is recorded.

Grinding

Vertical grinders and water grinders for bevels, primary edges and mirror polishing — the step most factories outsource and most buyers complain about.

Machining

CNC machining centre, drilling, pivot and spring-pin work for liner locks, frame locks and fixed blades.

Finishes

Stonewash, sandblast, titanium coating, etching, anodising and laser engraving — your logo cut into the blade, not printed on the box.

Handles & assembly

Wood, G10/FRN, die-cast and injection-moulded handles, assembled and inspected in-house.

Private label

Your steel spec, your handle material, your packaging and your branding — including low-volume trial runs.
