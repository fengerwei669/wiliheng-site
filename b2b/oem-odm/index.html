Customisation and how we work

What we can customise

  • Blade steel and hardness (5Cr15MoV, 440C, D2 and others on request)
  • Blade shape and grind, handle material and colour
  • Logo laser-engraved on blade, handle or both
  • Packaging: colour box, blister, protective sleeve, or your own artwork
  • Sheath, gift box and multi-pack combinations

OEM vs ODM

OEM: you bring the drawing, sample or specification and we build it. ODM: you pick from our existing models, we change blade finish, handle, colour and branding to make it yours. Both are normal for us; ODM is faster and cheaper for a first order.

Tooling and samples

Existing models need no tooling. A new shape or a new handle mould is quoted per project after we see the drawing. Sample cost and lead time are quoted per project — we would rather give you a real number than a marketing one.

What we need from you

Target price band, quantity per model, market and channel, logo file if branding is required, and any compliance documents your market requires.
