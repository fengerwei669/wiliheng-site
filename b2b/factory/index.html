What we do in-house

The factory route for a standard model runs 43 recorded process steps; each batch is logged in our own production system.

Blades & heat treatment

Blanking, annealing and tempering in our own furnaces, with hardness verified on our Rockwell tester and recorded per batch.

Grinding

Vertical and water grinders for bevels, primary edges and mirror polishing — the step most factories outsource and most buyers complain about.

Machining

CNC machining centres plus drilling, pivot and spring-pin work for liner locks, frame locks and fixed-blade kitchen knives.

Finishes

Stonewash, sandblast, titanium coating, etching, anodising and laser engraving — your logo cut into the blade, not printed on the box.

Handles & assembly

Wood, G10/FRN, die-cast and injection-moulded handles, assembled and inspected in-house.

Private label

Your steel spec, your handle material, your packaging and your branding — including low-volume trial runs.

Quality and export compliance

In-process checks

Hardness tested against the model's HRC specification, edge geometry checked after grinding, blade-to-handle fit and lock engagement checked at assembly.

Pre-shipment inspection

Carton data, quantity, packing and labelling are confirmed before the goods leave. Third-party inspection is welcome — arrange your own or ask us to cooperate with your agent.

Documents

Commercial invoice, packing list, FOB documents and the compliance certificates your market requires — tell us the destination and we will confirm what we can issue.

Arrange an inspection or a visit

Third-party inspection is welcome, and visiting buyers are shown the line — not a meeting room.
