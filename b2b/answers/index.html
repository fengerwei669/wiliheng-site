Buyer FAQ

Questions buyers ask before the first order

Yangjiang Wiliheng Knives Co., Ltd is an OEM/ODM cutlery and kitchenware factory in Yangjiang, Guangdong, making knives, shears, kitchen tools, silicone mats and 304 tableware for importers, distributors and private-label brands. Manufacturing since 2018, with 43 production steps and 40 machines in house (including 20 CNC machining centres); minimum order 500 pcs per model, quote within 24 hours on working days. This page is part of the company's B2B catalogue for importers, distributors and brand owners.

Short, factual answers about MOQ, lead times, steel, private label and shipping — written the way we answer them on the phone. New question added regularly: 25 of 39 published.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per model. That applies to stock models and to OEM/private-label runs. For mixed assortments we look at the total quantity instead of the per-line quantity, and a first order of a single model is completely normal — most of our distributor customers start with one or two models.

How long does production take?

Stock models ship in 20–30 days. OEM / private label with your own logo, handle and packaging takes 30–45 days after the sample is approved. The clock starts when the order and the artwork are confirmed, not when you first write to us.

Can I get samples before placing an order?

Yes. Samples are charged and normally credited against the production order. For private-label work the pre-production sample takes about one week after you approve the artwork, and production only starts once you sign that sample off.

How fast do you quote?

Within 24 hours on working days. Send the models or photo codes and the quantity you have in mind and you get factory pricing together with that model's specification sheet, carton data and packing list — not just a price on its own.

What do you make in house, and what is subcontracted?

Blade blanking, heat treatment (2 annealing and 2 tempering furnaces with Rockwell hardness testing), grinding (water and vertical grinders), CNC machining on 20 CNC centres, finishing and assembly all happen on our own site — 43 recorded process steps, nothing outsourced. That is why we can hold a hardness range per model instead of a vague 'stainless steel' claim.

Which steel do you use?

It depends on the model. Current lines run 5Cr15MoV at 52±2 HRC, 440C at 56±2 HRC and D2 at 58±2 HRC. For an OEM run we can work to your own steel specification within the hardness range the geometry supports; the specification sheet for the models you pick states the steel and hardness we will deliver.

What handle materials can you supply?

Wood (including riveted wooden handles), coloured resin/acrylic and composite handles — the ones you can see in our product photos. Handle material, colour and surface finish are all open for private-label runs; the per-model specification sheet names the handle we would use.

Do you do private label / OEM?

Yes — that is our core business, from 500 pcs per model: your steel, handle material, colour, laser-engraved logo, packaging and barcodes. You approve a pre-production sample before we run the order.

What packaging do you supply?

On current lines: colour box, colour box plus sheath, and colour box plus blister. Gift-boxed sets and retail-ready cartons are available. Carton dimensions, weights and the full packing list are issued with the quotation for the models you select.

How do I place an order or ask for prices?

Pick the designs you want from the product photos — every photo carries a reference code (for example W-4453) — or from the model list, and send us the codes and the quantities. We reply with pricing, specification and packing data for exactly those items.

Where is your factory, and can we visit?

Factory No. 2, Middle Section of Yudong Ninth Road, Yangdong Industrial Park, Yangjiang, Guangdong, China — Yangjiang is the cutlery cluster that supplies a large part of the world's kitchen knives. Buyers are welcome to visit, and we can meet at the Hong Kong or Guangzhou trade fairs.

Do you sell to consumers or single units?

No. We supply importers, distributors, wholesalers and private-label brands only. Orders below the per-model MOQ are not accepted and we do not ship single units — that is what keeps factory-direct pricing possible for our trade customers.

Can I use my own forwarder, and what are the shipping terms?

Yes. We quote FOB and work with your forwarder or ours. The packing list, carton data and shipping marks are confirmed with you before the goods leave the factory, so your customs broker has the documents before the container is loaded.

Where can I see your full range?

The downloadable PDF catalogue has all of our current product photos, each with its reference code, and the product-photo page on this site shows the same photos grouped by family. Quote the codes you like and we send the matching specification and pricing.

Which markets do you serve?

We ship worldwide and work most actively with buyers in the German-speaking market (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) and North America, alongside customers in the Middle East, Latin America and Asia. Our buyers are importers, distributors and brand owners rather than retail shops.

Are you a factory or a trading company?

We are the factory: Yangjiang Wiliheng Knives Co., Ltd, in Yangjiang, Guangdong, China, manufacturing since 2018. Cutting, heat treatment, grinding, CNC machining, finishing and assembly are done in our own plant (43 recorded steps, 20 CNC machining centres). There is no trading layer between you and the workshop.

Which knife types can you produce?

Folding and pocket knives, fixed-blade hunting and outdoor knives, kitchen knives, steak knife sets, shears and scissors, and kitchen gadgets (peelers, boards, sharpeners, silicone mats and trivets), plus 304 stainless tableware. If your model is outside these families, send the drawing and we will tell you honestly whether we can make it.

Can you make a model from our own drawing?

Yes. Send the drawing or a physical sample and we will quote after reviewing it. New shapes need tooling, so the quote covers that and the sample timeline starts when the drawing is confirmed. We only quote what we can actually produce in our own plant.

What steel options do you offer?

Current lines run 5Cr15MoV at 52±2 HRC, 440C at 56±2 HRC and D2 at 58±2 HRC. For OEM work we follow the steel you specify; if your target steel is outside what we run, we will say so rather than promise it.

Can you laser-engrave our logo?

Yes. Your logo can be laser-engraved on the blade and, where the material suits it, on the handle. Engraving is part of private-label production, so send the artwork and we will confirm the placement on the pre-production sample before the run starts.

Do you do private label (OEM/ODM)?

Yes — private label is most of what we do. From 500 pcs per model we produce with your steel, handle material and colour, your laser engraving, your packaging and your barcodes. You approve a pre-production sample and production starts after that.

What packaging options are there?

Colour box, box with sleeve, blister, hanging card and gift box, with your artwork and barcodes. Packaging dimensions and carton data are issued with the quotation so your forwarder can plan the load.

Can you print our barcodes and carton marks?

Yes. Barcodes (including your own EAN/UPC numbers) and carton marks are part of the packaging specification. Send the numbers and the shipping marks you need and they are confirmed before production.

How do you quote?

Send the models (or photo reference codes) and the quantity and you get prices within 24 hours on working days, together with the specification sheet and packaging and carton data. We quote FOB; give us your destination port and we will quote that too.

Do you quote FOB or other terms?

We quote FOB. We ship with your forwarder or ours, and the packing list and carton data are confirmed before the goods leave. Tell us the destination port and we will quote against it.

Still unsure about something? Send the models or photo codes and the quantities — you get the specification sheet, carton data and packing list with the price.
