Before you write

What makes a quote come back faster?

The five things we always need: model or reference, quantity, steel or target price, handle material, and branding method. Add your delivery country and we can quote freight at the same time.

Do you send a catalogue?

Yes. Ask for the outdoor knife catalogue and tell us which market you sell in — we will also point out which models in it suit that market's knife regulations and price expectations.

Can we ask for samples?

Yes. Sample knives are available for evaluation, and for custom programmes a pre-production sample is built to the agreed specification and must be approved before mass production.

What languages do you answer in?

English. Our team handles enquiries directly rather than through an agency, so you deal with the people who make the knives.
