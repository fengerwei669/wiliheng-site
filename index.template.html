Common questions from buyers

Are you a factory or a trading company?

We are the factory. Our plant is in Yangjiang, Guangdong, and the blades are blanked, heat treated, ground, machined and assembled on the same site. You are welcome to visit, or to arrange a third-party inspection.

What is your minimum order quantity?

__MOQ_MIN__ pcs per model. A first order of a single model is normal; for mixed assortments we look at the total quantity.

Do you make private label — our brand?

Yes. Your logo can be laser-engraved on the blade and the handle, and we pack in your own artwork: colour boxes, blisters or protective sleeves.

Do you sell to consumers?

No. We supply importers, distributors and brands only — orders below the MOQ for a model are not accepted, and we do not ship single units.

How do you quote and ship?

Send the models and quantities and you get factory pricing within 24 hours. We quote FOB and ship with your forwarder or ours, with the packing list and carton data confirmed before the goods leave.
